Privacy Policy — ReelReminder

Effective date: 2026-11-28

1. Local-Only Storage

ReelReminder is a local-only Android app. All saved Instagram reel links, notes, collections, watch logs, and optional user-picked cover images are stored exclusively on your device. We operate no backend servers and collect no accounts, analytics, or personal data.

2. Permissions Used

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used solely to display your scheduled reel reminders.
  • Exact Alarms (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM): Used to fire reminders on time.
  • Internet (INTERNET): Used only when you open the optional in-app Instagram embed player. No background network calls or scraping are performed.
  • Photo Picker: Optional cover images are picked via Android's system Photo Picker without broad storage permissions.

3. Data Deletion & Backup

You can export or import your local data as JSON, or wipe all local reels, covers, watch history, alarms, and WebView cache at any time via Settings > Data & Privacy > Delete all my data.
